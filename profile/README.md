## Hey, we're Match Group 👋

![Match Group: the company behind Tinder, Hinge, Match, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish and more](./matchgroup.png)

We build the apps where people meet. Our mission is to **spark meaningful connections for every single person worldwide**, and we take that literally.

Our apps facilitate roughly **500 million matches every month**. Behind them are **2,000+ people in 20 offices** around the world, writing the code, training the models, and keeping the community safe. And with about **1 in 3 marriages now starting on an app**, the stakes feel pretty real to us. 💘

### 💞 One company, a lot of ways to connect

Connection isn't one-size-fits-all, so we don't build one app. We build a family of them:

- 🔥 **[Tinder](https://tinder.com)**: the world's best-known dating app, built for exploring and meeting new people
- 🌹 **[Hinge](https://hinge.co)**: "designed to be deleted," for people looking for something real
- ❤️ **[Match](https://www.match.com)**, **[The League](https://www.theleague.com)**, **[Pairs](https://www.pairs.lv)** and **[Meetic](https://www.meetic.fr)**: built for clarity and commitment
- 🌈 **[HER](https://weareher.com)**, **[BLK](https://www.blk-app.com)**, **[Upward](https://www.upwardapp.com)** and **[OurTime](https://www.ourtime.com)**: created around shared culture and community
- 🌍 **[OkCupid](https://www.okcupid.com)**, **[Plenty of Fish](https://www.pof.com)** and **[Azar](https://azarlive.com)**: connecting people across dozens of countries and languages

### 🚀 What we've been shipping

We've been moving fast this year, and a lot of it is powered by AI and by sharing technology across our brands (internally we call this **One MG**).

**On Tinder**

- **Chemistry**: an AI-driven way to match. A short Q&A, plus an optional Camera Roll Scan (only with your permission), helps Tinder understand your personality and what matters to you.
- **Modes**: lower-pressure ways to connect, like **Double Date**, **Music Mode** and **Astrology Mode**.
- **Events**: helps people find things to do and meet in real life. It started as a pilot in Los Angeles and is now rolling out city by city.
- **Face Check™**: a facial liveness check that confirms someone is a real person. Where it's live, interactions with bad actors have dropped **more than 50%**.
- **"Are You Sure?"**: a gentle nudge that flags potentially harmful language before someone hits send.
- A full rebrand, plus new search, a redesigned chat, a rebuilt Likes You tab, and new profiles.

**On Hinge**

- **Convo Starters**: AI-powered prompts that help people get past "hey" and into a real conversation.
- **Direct to Date**: an experiment to make intentions clear and get people from chatting to an actual date faster.
- **Face Check™**: bringing the same trust and safety technology from Tinder to Hinge.
- Growing fast internationally, with global monthly active users up **13% year over year** and strong momentum in Europe and Latin America.

### 🛠️ The problems we work on

If you like hard problems with a human on the other end, you'll feel at home here:

- **Recommendations at massive scale**: ranking and matching for many millions of people, where "relevant" means a real spark, not just a click
- **Trust & safety**: machine learning, identity verification, and real-time detection that keep bad actors out, ideally before anyone notices they were there
- **Responsible AI**: building features people trust, guided by our [commitment on AI](https://mtch.com/trust-safety/our-commitment-to-ai/) and our [privacy principles](https://mtch.com/trust-safety/privacy/) (we don't sell your data)
- **Real-time and global**: chat, video and events across dozens of languages and markets
